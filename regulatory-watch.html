Regulatory watch

The CRA is not a 2027 event; it moves monthly. This log records what already changed under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and the fixed statutory dates ahead, each entry dated and tied to its primary source. Entries carry the as-of date of my last verification.

The log

What has happened

10 Nov 2026

M/606 standardisation request amended: +3 months

The Commission (document C(2026)7861) extended the delivery deadlines for all vertical and some horizontal harmonised standards by three months. A public admission that the standards layer will not be finished as the December 2027 application date approaches. The request M/606 itself (document C(2025)618) commissions 41 standards: 15 horizontal, including vulnerability handling, and 26 vertical, product-specific ones.

Consequence for planning: presumption of conformity (Art 27) needs harmonised standards cited in the OJEU; until they exist, self-assessment against Annex I directly is the working route for early movers.

Sources: Commission documents C(2025)618, C(2026)7861; verified in my research report, 18 Nov 2026.

15 Oct 2026

Commission open-source page confirms the FOSS settlement

FOSS is in scope only when made available on the market in a commercial activity; unmonetised publication is not commercial and individual contributors are never responsible. The open source software steward (Art 24) is a new legal category: legal persons systematically sustaining FOSS for commercial use must run a public cybersecurity policy, cooperate with authorities, and report selected Art 14 duties from 11 December 2027 (Art 24(3)) — but stewards are exempt from administrative fines (Art 64(10)).

Source: Commission open source page, updated 15 Oct 2026; reproduced in my research report, 18 Nov 2026.

11 Sep 2026

Article 14 vulnerability reporting went live; ENISA SRP launched

An actively exploited vulnerability or severe incident in a product on the EU market now starts a 24-hour early-warning clock and a 72-hour main notification, submitted through ENISA’s Single Reporting Platform. The duty also covers products shipped before the application date.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 Art 14; ENISA SRP launch; verified 18 Nov 2026.

11 Jun 2026

Notified bodies became notifiable

Member States had to designate their notifying authorities by this date; ENISA has published the technical competence requirements for conformity-assessment bodies. The Commission’s implementation milestone expects a sufficient number of notified bodies by 11 December 2026.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, implementation timeline; verified 18 Nov 2026.

28 Nov 2025

Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392 drew the category boundaries

The implementing regulation fixes precisely which products fall into which Annex III/IV class. Classification is the first deliverable of any CRA program: it decides the conformity route, from internal self-assessment to EU-type examination.

Source: Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392 of 28 November 2025; verified 18 Nov 2026.

10 Dec 2024

Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 entered into force

The Cyber Resilience Act entered into force; the long runway to full application on 11 December 2027 started, with reporting duties applying earlier and supporting infrastructure (notified bodies, standards, the SRP) built along the way.

Source: OJ L series, 20.11.2024, via the Publications Office.

Fixed by the statute

What is coming

11 Dec 2026

Notified-bodies milestone

The Commission’s implementation milestone expects a sufficient number of notified bodies to exist. Class II and critical products that need third-party conformity assessment should queue early — capacity, not law, becomes the bottleneck.

11 Dec 2027

Full application

Annex I essential requirements, CE marking, support-period duties, and the Art 24(3) steward reporting duties apply. Related: the Commission FAQ notes the CRA takes over the radio-equipment cybersecurity requirements (currently EN 18031 cited for the RED) from this date; certificates under other schemes keep validity until 11 June 2028.
